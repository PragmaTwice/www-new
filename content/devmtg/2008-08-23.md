## About

This was the first LLVM gathering held on August 23, 2008 in Tokyo, Japan.

At the gathering, attendees introduced LLVM and explored its use in various
projects. Around 40 people attended. See also the archived
[Google Groups site](http://groups.google.co.jp/group/llvm_study/web/1-llvm-gathering-in-japan).
