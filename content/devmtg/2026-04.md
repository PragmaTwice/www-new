## About

The EuroLLVM Developers' Meeting is a gathering of the entire LLVM Project
community. The conference is organized by the LLVM Foundation and volunteers
within the LLVM community. Developers and users of LLVM, Clang, and related
subprojects attend talks, impromptu discussions, and networking sessions.

### What can you expect at an LLVM Developers' Meeting?

- **Keynotes** are 40-45 minute talks that provide an overview of a topic or
  project and often capture its history and impact.
- **Technical Talks** are 20-30 minute sessions covering core infrastructure,
  LLVM subprojects, and uses of LLVM in academia and industry.
- **Tutorials** are 50-60 minute sessions that explore a technical topic in
  depth, with examples and explanations.
- **Lightning Talks** are fast 5 minute introductions to a project or topic.
- **Quick Talks** are 10 minute talks that go deeper than a lightning talk.
- **Student Technical Talks** let graduate and undergraduate students present
  their work using LLVM.
- **Panels** are guided discussions with developers and audience questions.

### Who attends?

- Active developers of projects in the LLVM umbrella.
- Anyone interested in using LLVM projects as part of another project.
- Students and researchers.
- Compiler, programming language, and runtime enthusiasts.
- Sponsors and partners using LLVM technology in their products.

For future announcements or questions, visit the
[LLVM Discourse forums](https://discourse.llvm.org/) and the
[eurollvm tag](https://discourse.llvm.org/tag/eurollvm).
